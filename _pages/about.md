I use **machine learning and data science to find where and when ore deposits form**. My work links plate tectonic reconstructions and geodynamic models to the deposit record, so that prospectivity models can predict mineralisation through deep time, not just across today's map.

At ANU, I work with Prof. Rhodri Davies in the Computational & Observational Geodynamics group. I am building the machine learning framework for **[GEM-Cu](https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/projects/gem-cu-geodynamic-environments-for-mineralisation-of-cu-copper/)** (Geodynamic Environments for Mineralisation of Copper). GEM-Cu is an industry project with Rio Tinto, The University of British Columbia and Imperial College London, which brings mantle convection models into copper exploration.

Before ANU, I spent five years as a Research Associate in the [EarthByte Group](https://www.earthbyte.org/) at The University of Sydney with Prof. Dietmar Müller. There I developed 2D and spatio-temporal prospectivity frameworks for the [PLATO](https://www.earthbyte.org/plato-plate-tectonics-and-ore-deposits/) and [STELLAR](https://www.earthbyte.org/stellar/) industry projects, lectured undergraduate courses and supervised research students. I hold a PhD in Mining Engineering (Mineral Exploration) from Amirkabir University of Technology.

**Research interests**

- Spatio-temporal mineral prospectivity modelling (porphyry copper and sediment-hosted systems)
- 3D and 2D prospectivity modelling where labelled data are scarce
- Geophysical and geochemical data analysis, including anomaly detection and crustal thickness estimation
- Geological remote sensing with computer vision and deep learning

I am a recipient of the 2025 **ASEG Early Achievement Award** and the 2025 **COSPAR Outstanding Paper Award for Young Scientists**.
