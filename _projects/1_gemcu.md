**Partners:** Rio Tinto, The University of British Columbia, Imperial College London
**Role:** Research Fellow, Research School of Earth Sciences, ANU

GEM-Cu asks which deep-Earth conditions create fertile environments for copper. I am developing a machine learning framework for spatio-temporal mineral prospectivity modelling that combines plate reconstructions, subduction parameters and time-dependent mantle convection models with the global record of dated porphyry deposits.
