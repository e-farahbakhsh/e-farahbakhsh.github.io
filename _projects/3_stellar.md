**Partners:** Datarock, Lithodat, BHP, Curtin University (with the EarthByte Group)
**Role:** Research Associate

STELLAR develops spatio-temporal exploration tools for critical and base metals. My work covered machine learning prospectivity models for critical minerals where known deposits are few and negative examples are unreliable. It also included unsupervised detection of craton structures from geophysical data and seismic tomography.
