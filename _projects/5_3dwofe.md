3DWofE is an open-source package for three-dimensional weights of evidence modelling. It lets explorers integrate drill-hole and geophysical evidence in 3D to target concealed, deep-seated deposits. It grew out of my PhD on 3D modelling of ore deposits by integrating data layers.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/e-farahbakhsh/3DWofE)
