PLATO

PLAte Tectonics and Ore deposits (University of Sydney, 2021–2026)

Partners: Industry partners with the EarthByte Group, The University of Sydney Role: Research Associate

PLATO links the evolution of tectonic plates to the formation and preservation of ore deposits. I built machine learning workflows that turn plate-kinematic and subduction-zone attributes into spatio-temporal prospectivity maps for porphyry systems, from the New Guinea–Solomon Islands region to the Tethyan belt and the full 1.8-billion-year record.
