Remote sensing and computer vision

Deep learning for mapping alteration, lithology and lineaments from satellite data

I develop computer vision and deep learning methods for geological remote sensing. These include convolutional neural networks for mapping alteration zones, stacked autoencoders for lithological mapping, and an automated framework for extracting tectonic lineaments from optical imagery. The CNN alteration-mapping paper received the 2025 COSPAR Outstanding Paper Award for Young Scientists.

Key papers: (Farahbakhsh et al., 2025; Shirmard et al., 2022; Shirmard et al., 2022; Nagar et al., 2024; Farahbakhsh et al., 2020)
